# Dive

Dive is the Computer Science chapter's committee for getting more people to apply to the Computer Science programme at KTH, with a special focus on a more even gender balance in the programme.

We visit high schools and technical foundation year classes (tekniskt basår) and tell them what studying Computer Science at KTH is really like. We show the chapter's inclusive values and our own members' experiences, so that more students, especially women and non-binary people, feel confident applying.

## What we do

- Give presentations for high school classes and technical foundation year classes
- Run social media aimed at future students
- Put together information for those who are about to choose what to study
- Work together with KTH and with organisations that have the same goal

The committee is divided into a visiting group, which gives the presentations, and a communications group, which takes care of the website, social media and graphic profile.
