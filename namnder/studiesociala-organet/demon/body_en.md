**Welcome to Data Et Medias Orchestral Network!** Want to represent the chapter at live gigs around Stockholm, start a band and practice with your friends, or just have a place to learn that musical instrument you've been procrastinating? Then this is the place to be!

## JAM SESSIONS

Looking for something a bit more casual? Drop by one of our jam sessions!
*But what is a jam sesh*, you might ask?

Every two weeks or so, the D Chapter's Archdemon and Me Chapter's Maestro book a rehearsal room at **Malmskillnadsgatan 49** and host an open so-called *jam session* where everyone from D or Me are welcome to come and practice their favorite songs with us, improvise and cook up new musical ideas or take the first step on a new instrument. We have something for all levels of skill! 

Each time we plan on having a jam we'll send a message in our [Discord server](https://dsekt.se/demon-en) about a week beforehand, and if you want to attend a jam sesh just join the server and send a message in the accompanying thread with the instrument you'll be playing (if any) so that we know how many people will be coming to the jam session!

## START YOUR BAND HERE

Interested in starting a band with your friends from D and Me? We can help you with that! Join our club with your band, and you'll be able to book rehearsal rooms and borrow our instruments, **free of charge!** [It's as easy as filling in this form](https://forms.gle/zG4YNggRUTVmnNnf9). NOTE that your band must be majority students from the D and/or Me chapters!

## OUR STUFF

If you join DEMON as a band, or even as a solo artist, then you can borrow our stuff! The chapter owns a digital and an acoustic drum set, an acoustic and an electric guitar, an electric bass guitar and a digital piano. Everything is free to borrow, so don't hesitate to try new stuff! [You can find more information about how to borrow things here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1GRiPdKTYaj6bzlVvcLsrslecyOaz_GepB5rbgAWAsAg/edit?usp=sharing).
